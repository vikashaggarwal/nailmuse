✦ Common Questions

Gel Nails FAQs

How much does a gel manicure cost in Mumbai?
At Nail Muse (Malad West), a Gel Manicure costs ₹899 and lasts 2–3 weeks. We also offer Basic Manicure at ₹499 and Luxury Manicure at ₹1,299. All include nail shaping, cuticle care, and finishing.
How long do gel nails last in Mumbai's climate?
Gel nails at Nail Muse last 2–3 weeks even in Mumbai's humidity. We use premium UV-cured gel polish formulated for tropical climates. With basic care (avoiding harsh chemicals, wearing gloves while cleaning), they can last up to 4 weeks.
Is gel nail removal safe?
Yes, when done professionally. At Nail Muse, we use a safe soak-off method with gentle acetone, foil wraps, and a nourishing conditioning treatment after removal. We never scrape or forcefully remove gel — this protects your natural nails. Removal costs ₹299.
Can I get gel nails on short or bitten nails?
Absolutely! Gel polish works beautifully on short nails. For very bitten or weak nails, we recommend our Hard Gel Overlay (₹1,299) which strengthens the nail while looking completely natural.
Do you have a gel nail salon in Malad West?
Yes! Nail Muse is located at Shop No 5, Bhoomi Classic, New Link Rd, Malad West, Mumbai 400064. Open all 7 days, 11am–9:30pm. Book online or WhatsApp +91 88793 36671.
