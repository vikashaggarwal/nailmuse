Nail Extension FAQs

How much do nail extensions cost in Mumbai?
At Nail Muse Malad West: Gel Extensions Full Set = ₹2,200 | Acrylic Extensions = ₹1,800 | Infill = ₹999 | Removal = ₹399. All include gel polish colour of your choice.
Gel extensions vs acrylic — which should I choose?
Choose gel if you want the most natural look, flexibility, and minimum nail damage — ideal for first-timers and everyday wear. Choose acrylic if you want maximum length, dramatic shapes (stiletto, coffin), and extra durability for events and weddings.
How long do nail extensions last?
Nail extensions last 3–4 weeks before needing an infill. With proper care — not using nails as tools, avoiding prolonged water exposure, wearing gloves when cleaning — your extensions will stay beautiful longer.

Do nail extensions damage your natural nails?
Not when applied and removed professionally. At Nail Muse, we prep nails gently, never over-file, and use safe soak-off removal with conditioning treatment. Our technique protects your natural nail health throughout the process.
Can I get nail art on extensions?
Absolutely! Extensions are a perfect canvas for nail art. We offer hand-painted designs, 3D art, chrome, ombré, and more. Just mention your design inspiration when booking and our artists will create it for you.
