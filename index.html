Frequently Asked Questions

What nail services does Nail Muse offer in Mumbai?
Nail Muse offers gel manicure (from ₹899), nail art (from ₹150/nail), gel & acrylic extensions (from ₹1,800), bridal nail packages (from ₹8,500), pedicure services, and seasonal nail art — all in Mumbai.
How much does a gel manicure cost at Nail Muse?
A Gel Manicure costs ₹899 and lasts 2–3 weeks chip-free. We also offer Basic Manicure at ₹499 and Luxury Manicure at ₹1,299. All services include cuticle care and finishing.
How do I book a nail appointment in Mumbai?
Book online at nailmuse.in/booking.html in 60 seconds — pick your service, date & time. Or WhatsApp us at +91 88793 36671. We confirm within 2 hours.
Does Nail Muse do bridal nail packages?
Yes! Our Bridal Full Package starts at ₹8,500 — includes design consultation, trial session, gel extensions, custom nail art, and bridal mani-pedi. Bridal slots are limited — book early!
What are Nail Muse's business hours?
We are open Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM. Closed on Sundays. Find our studio in Mumbai →
