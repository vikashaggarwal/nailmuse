Where exactly is Nail Muse in Malad West?

What are the opening hours?
Nail Muse Malad West is open all 7 days — Monday to Sunday, 11:00 AM to 9:30 PM. Walk-ins are welcome anytime. For peak hours (weekends and evenings), we recommend booking online to secure your slot.

Walk-ins are always welcome! However, for specific services like bridal packages, nail art, or extensions — we recommend booking online or WhatsApp to guarantee your preferred time slot, especially on weekends.
