✦ How It All Began

Where Art Meets Nails

Nail Muse was founded in 2016 from a simple belief: your nails are a canvas, and every canvas deserves a true artist. Our founder — passionate about the intersection of beauty and storytelling — set out to create Mumbai's most intimate, artistic nail experience.

Nearly a decade later, that belief has touched 20,000+ clients. We've created hundreds of thousands of unique nail designs, witnessed over 1,000 bridal transformations, and built a community that celebrates self-expression through nail art. As we approach our 10th anniversary in 2026, we're humbled and grateful for every single client who trusted us with their nails.

Every client who walks through our doors is treated as both a muse and a collaborator. We listen, we sketch, we perfect — until your nails tell exactly the story you envision.

✦ What We Stand For

Our Three Pillars

🧴

Hygiene First

Sterilised tools for every client. We use hospital-grade autoclave sterilisation, single-use files and buffers, and UV-sanitised equipment. Your safety is our foundation — not an afterthought.

🎨

Artistry Always

We see every set of nails as a unique canvas. We invest in continuous training, keep up with global trends, and never settle for "just good enough." Every brush stroke is intentional.

⭐

Consistent Excellence

Our standards don't fluctuate. Whether it's your first visit or your fiftieth, you'll receive the same meticulous care, the same warm welcome, and the same commitment to perfection.
