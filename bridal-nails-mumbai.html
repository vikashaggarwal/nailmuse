✦ Bridal FAQ

Wedding Nail Questions

How much does a bridal nail package cost in Mumbai?
The Bridal Full Package at Nail Muse starts at ₹8,500 and includes consultation, trial session, gel extensions, custom nail art, bridal manicure & pedicure. Mehendi Nail Art starts at ₹3,500. Bridesmaid packages at ₹1,299/person (10% off for groups of 3+).
How far in advance should I book bridal nails?
We recommend booking 2–3 months before your wedding, especially during wedding season (October–February). Bridal slots are limited. WhatsApp us at +91 88793 36671 to check availability for your wedding date.
What nail designs are best for Indian weddings?
Popular choices for Indian brides: White/nude French tips with crystal gems (classic and photos beautifully), Mehendi-inspired hand-painted art (traditional and unique), Gold foil designs (complements Indian jewellery), and Soft pink gel extensions with gold accents. We customise every set to match your wedding outfit.
Can you match nail art to my bridal outfit?
Absolutely! Share photos of your lehenga, saree, or wedding outfit during consultation. We colour-match and design nails that complement your outfit, jewellery, and mehendi. Our bridal artists have done nails for all styles — traditional, fusion, and contemporary.
Do you service brides in Malad and nearby areas?
Yes! Nail Muse is at Shop No 5, Bhoomi Classic, New Link Rd, Malad West, Mumbai 400064 — easily accessible from Malad, Borivali, Kandivali, Goregaon, and Andheri. Open 7 days, 11am–9:30pm. Book online or WhatsApp for bridal consultation.
